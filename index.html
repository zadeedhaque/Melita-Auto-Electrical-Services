What we do

Auto electrical work, start to finish

Cars, 4WDs, vans and campervans. Book in, drop it off, and we will call you with a clear quote before any work starts.

Diagnostics & fault finding

Warning lights, intermittent faults, power drains and wiring problems traced to the source with proper diagnostic gear.

Batteries & dual battery systems

Battery testing and replacement, plus dual battery and house battery setups for 4WDs, SUVs and campervans.

Lighting repairs

Headlights, brake lights, indicators and interior lighting. Globe, wiring and module faults sorted.

Dashcam hard-wiring

Clean, hidden installs wired to the fuse box so your dashcam powers on with the car, with parking mode where supported.

Starting & charging

Starter motor and alternator testing, repair and replacement when the car will not crank or the battery will not hold charge.

Accessory installs

Turbo timers, reversing cameras, driving lights, power outlets and other 12V accessories fitted neatly and safely.
